AI picking

A language model reads what was said at the loudest moments, picks the best ones, trims them and writes the titles. Without it, Cliphound ranks moments by loudness and hype words.

The AI uses this to decide which moments fit your channel.

Streamers

Added the first time you paste one of their links. Turn on permission once a streamer has said yes to clipping.

Storage

Cliphound keeps each stream's audio for 7 days so you can clip it again without re-downloading. Older audio is cleared when the app starts.
